The Lighthouse on the Silent Lake

On a chilly October evening, Jonas entered his grandfather's abandoned house and found an old key on the kitchen table. Beside it lay a note: "When the light by the lake shines again, you will understand."

Jonas knew the lighthouse on the opposite shore. It had been out of service for twenty years. Yet that night he saw a faint glow behind the trees. He put on his jacket and set off.

The path was damp from the rain. The wind whistled between the stones, but the closer Jonas came to the tower, the more clearly he heard a quiet ticking. The door was locked. The key from the kitchen fit perfectly.

Inside stood an old clock whose hands were running backward. On a table lay his grandfather's logbook. The final page contained only one sentence: "Some memories need a light to help us find them again."

Jonas climbed the narrow staircase. At the top, he found a small lamp and a photograph showing him as a child beside his grandfather. When he switched on the lamp, it did not illuminate the lake. Instead, its light revealed the faded words on the back of the photograph: "Never forget to come home."

By the next morning, the light in the tower had gone out. But Jonas kept the key. He finally understood why his grandfather had never forgotten the way to the lighthouse.
